# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Accept responsibility, apologize to those affected by mistakes, and learn
  from the experience.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project.

Harassment, personal attacks, trolling, discriminatory language, sexualized
attention, and publishing others' private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/beremaran). Reports will be reviewed promptly and
fairly. The maintainer may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently ban contributors for behavior considered
inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
